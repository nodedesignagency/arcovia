The story comes before the spreadsheet

Every mandate starts with the narrative. We build the investor memo, teaser and deck as one positioning system, match the founder’s thesis to specific investor criteria, and stress-test the story against real investor objections before outreach begins.

GENESIS, our capital platform

GENESIS is Arcovia’s proprietary investor targeting and outreach platform. It supports our senior team; it never replaces judgment.

  1. 01

    Investor targeting

    AI-assisted matching on thesis, sector, stage and check size. A qualified target list in 2–3 days instead of 2–4 weeks.

  2. 02

    Personalised outreach

    Sequences mapped to each investor’s thesis and portfolio. No generic blasts.

  3. 03

    Engagement analytics

    Real-time signals on replies and meeting acceptance, and a continuous ranking of investor likelihood.

  4. 04

    Pipeline conversion

    Qualified investors routed to founder meetings with objections handled in advance, plus warm introductions through our 200+ active relationships.

Performance metrics are based on internal data and management estimates. Technology does not guarantee transaction success.
