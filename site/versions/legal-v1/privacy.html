Who we are

This policy explains how Arcovia legal entity (“Arcovia”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information collected through arcovia.io. Under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, Arcovia legal entity is the controller of that information.

Our offices are at 2029 Century Park East, Los Angeles, CA 90067 and 450 Ave. 200, San Juan, Puerto Rico 00901.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us and a little about how you found us.

  • When you write to us: your name and email, your company, firm or organisation, your role, and what you tell us about your raise, your mandate or your enquiry. If you apply to join the team, also your LinkedIn profile and current firm.
  • About your visit: the page you arrived on and the campaign that brought you (UTM tags), saved with your enquiry.
  • Only if you accept analytics: how pages are used, counted without identifying you. See the Cookie Policy.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and pass your note to the right person on our team.
  • To prepare for a possible engagement and keep a record of our conversations.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns bring people to us.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not make automated decisions about you, and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask for them.

Legal bases

Under the GDPR we rely on your consent (for analytics, which you can withdraw at any time), our legitimate interest in replying to enquiries and running our business, steps you ask us to take before an agreement, and our legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • GoHighLevel, which runs our contact forms and client records.
  • Framer, which hosts this website.
  • Our analytics provider, Plausible or Google Analytics, and only if you accept analytics.
  • LemVega Capital and other licensed partners, where a transaction requires it to confirm.
  • Professional advisors and authorities, where the law requires it.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where information leaves the EU or the UK, we rely on standard contractual clauses or another approved safeguard.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for retention period, and longer only where the law or an active engagement requires it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive a copy to take elsewhere. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

In the EU and the UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act you can ask what personal information we collect, use and disclose, ask us to delete or correct it, and opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No website or email is completely secure, so please do not send confidential documents through the contact form.

Children

This website is for professionals and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions or requests about your information, write to privacy email, or to us at 2029 Century Park East, Los Angeles, CA 90067.
